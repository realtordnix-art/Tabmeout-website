Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Tab Me Out LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Tab Me Out mobile application and related services (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our Service.

This policy is provided in compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (including 5.1.1) and Google Play policies. It applies to the application once released and to any data processed through this website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through use of the Service:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, username, profile details, and authentication credentials.
  • Profile and content: Photos, videos, messages, gift descriptions, and other user-generated content you choose to share.
  • Payment and transaction data: Payment method tokens (processed by third-party processors such as Stripe), transaction amounts, recipient information, and gift/tab history. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Device and usage data: Device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and usage analytics (with consent where required).
  • Location data: Approximate or precise location only when you grant permission and it is relevant to a feature (e.g., nearby venues).

We practice data minimization and only collect data necessary for the core functionality of social networking, messaging, gifting, and payments.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process gifts, tabs, and electronic payments
  • Enable messaging and profile features
  • Authenticate users and prevent fraud
  • Communicate with you about the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only as necessary:

  • With payment processors (e.g., Stripe) to complete transactions. These parties are required to protect data to the same or equal standard.
  • With service providers who assist in hosting, analytics, or customer support under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • With your explicit consent for any other purpose.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share data with third-party AI providers for training without explicit consent.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service or comply with legal obligations. Transaction records may be retained longer for financial and tax compliance.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through the in-app account deletion flow (required by Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v)) or by contacting us at support@tabmeout.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.

You may also withdraw consent for optional data collection (such as analytics or location) through device settings or in-app controls.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. International Users

Information may be processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to transfer of your information to the U.S.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.
