About Tab Me Out

Tab Me Out LLC is the legal entity behind the Tab Me Out application. Our focus is building a platform that enables social gifting, QR-based tab sharing, and related features for venues, users, and businesses.

This website serves as the official company presence for Tab Me Out LLC, providing access to our privacy policy, support resources, and terms of service. It will continue to host legal and support pages as the application is completed and expanded.

Application Overview

Planned Core Functionality

  • Social networking and user profiles
  • Messaging and private media sharing
  • Person-to-person gifting and electronic payments
  • QR-based tab sharing for stores, venues, and services
  • Support for unbanked users via platform wallets

The full application is under active development. This site remains intentionally minimal and will be expanded once the desktop and mobile experiences are complete.
