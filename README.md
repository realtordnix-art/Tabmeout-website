# Tab Me Out Official Website (Minimal)

Static site created for Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console enrollment requirements.

## Purpose
- Public, functional website on the organization domain (tabmeout.app)
- Privacy Policy URL (required by Apple Guideline 5.1.1 and Google Play)
- Support URL (required by Apple Guideline 1.5)
- Associated with Tab Me Out LLC (legal entity)

## Pages
- index.html – Home / company overview
- privacy-policy.html – Full privacy policy
- support.html – Contact form + FAQs (Support URL)
- terms.html – Terms of Service

## Deployment
1. Upload all files to the root of your hosting for tabmeout.app (e.g., GoDaddy).
2. Ensure HTTPS is enabled.
3. Set support@tabmeout.app as the contact email (configure email forwarding or inbox).
4. Update any placeholder details (address, etc.) if needed.
5. Use these URLs in App Store Connect and Play Console:
   - Privacy Policy: https://tabmeout.app/privacy-policy.html
   - Support: https://tabmeout.app/support.html
